Accessibility Statement

For TheStatusQ. Last reviewed 29 July 2026.

TheStatusQ is meant to be usable by everyone, including people who browse with a screen reader, navigate by keyboard, use magnification, or need reduced motion.

The standard we build to

This site was built to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1, Level AA. That is the benchmark used in most accessibility settlements and the one public bodies are held to, so it is the one we target.

We do not describe the site as certified, because no such certification exists for a website. What we can tell you is exactly what was checked and when.

How it was tested

  • Automated testing with axe-core against the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rulesets, at desktop, tablet and small phone widths.
  • A manual keyboard pass covering focus order, focus visibility, and the ability to reach and leave every interactive element.
  • Colour contrast checked by calculation rather than by eye, for text, buttons and interface elements.
  • A check that the page reflows without horizontal scrolling at 320 pixels and at 200 percent zoom.
  • A check that motion respects the reduced-motion setting, and that anything that loops can be stopped.

Last full review: 29 July 2026.

Known limitations

Being straight about the gaps is more useful than claiming there are none.

  • Some pages load fonts and scripts from outside services. If one of those fails to load, the page still works and stays readable, but the typeface will differ.
  • Content embedded from other services, such as video players, booking tools or social feeds, is controlled by those providers. Where an embed creates a barrier we offer another route to the same thing.

Tell us if something blocks you

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible to use, tell us and we will fix it. Describe the page and what happened, and we will reply within five business days.

Email hello@thestatusq.io.

If you need something on this site in another format, such as a document read out or supplied as plain text, ask and we will provide it at no cost.
